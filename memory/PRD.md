# TUGMA — Product Requirements Document

## Product

**TUGMA — Compliance You Can Prove**

**Category:** Continuous Payment Control Intelligence

TUGMA is a B2B RegTech platform designed to help regulated payment operators continuously test actual payment operations against defined controls, identify exceptions, manage remediation, collect evidence, and produce verifiable evidence packages.

The initial prototype focuses on payment collections and merchant settlement operations in the Philippine regulatory context.

---

# Product Objective

The core question TUGMA addresses is:

> Did the actual payment operation follow the required control, and can we prove what happened?
